Page-make Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information: when you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple user identifier (User ID) and email (if you choose to share it).
1.2 Uploaded content: the zip or HTML/CSS/JS files you upload, including their extracted contents.
1.3 Usage logs: timestamps and results of operations such as upload, deploy and link refresh, used for troubleshooting.
1.4 Feedback: messages and reports you submit via Settings > Feedback.

2. How We Use Information

2.1 To provide the static site hosting service: extracting archives, checking your code, and generating public links.
2.2 To troubleshoot issues and keep the service safe (e.g. banning abusive accounts, taking down violating projects).
2.3 We do not use your information for advertising and do not sell it to any third party.

3. Where Information Is Stored

3.1 Your files and project data are stored on the servers of the hosting provider you choose when creating a project (EdgeOne or Cloudflare), or on your self-hosted server.
3.2 Please note: public links are accessible to anyone while valid. Do not upload files containing sensitive information.
3.3 When you delete a project, its files are deleted immediately. Projects taken down for policy violations keep their files for 7 days as evidence before deletion.

4. Information Sharing

4.1 Hosting providers (EdgeOne, Cloudflare and other infrastructure providers) necessarily have access to your file content in order to serve it.
4.2 We do not disclose your information to any other third party unless required by law.

5. Tracking and Advertising

This app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Your Rights

6.1 You can delete your projects and their files at any time within the app.
6.2 To delete your account and all associated data, contact us via Settings > Feedback.
6.3 You can sign out at any time (Settings > Sign Out).

7. Protection of Minors

This service is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information. Minors should use the service under guardian supervision.

8. Policy Updates

If this policy changes materially, we will update this document in the app and update the effective date.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us via Settings > Feedback in the app.
