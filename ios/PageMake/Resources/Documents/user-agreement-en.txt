Page-make User Agreement

Effective date: October 2026

1. Service Description

Page-make is a static site hosting tool: you upload a zip or individual HTML/CSS/JS files, and we automatically extract them, check your code, and generate a publicly accessible link. The service is free for signed-in users.

2. Accounts

2.1 This service supports Apple, Google, administrator-issued VIP accounts, and guest access. Accounts are for personal use only.
2.2 You are responsible for all activity under your account.

3. Content Rules

Uploaded content must comply with applicable laws and public order. The following are prohibited:

3.1 Fraud and phishing content;
3.2 Pornographic content;
3.3 Violent or terrorist content;
3.4 Malware and viruses;
3.5 Content infringing intellectual property or other lawful rights;
3.6 Any other unlawful content.

4. Enforcement

4.1 Violating projects will be taken down (link shows "taken down"); files are kept for 7 days as evidence, then deleted.
4.2 Violating accounts are banned by level: first minor violation — 1 day; repeat violations — 1 week; fraud or phishing — 1 month; pornography, terrorism, or malware — permanent.
4.3 Reports are reviewed manually. If you believe an action was taken in error, you may appeal via the feedback channel.

5. User Responsibility

5.1 You are solely responsible for the legality and copyright of your uploaded content.
5.2 Public links are open to everyone. Do not upload passwords, keys, or other sensitive information.

6. Service Availability

6.1 This is a free service with no guarantee of continuous availability; it may be interrupted or changed due to maintenance, failures, or platform policy changes.
6.2 Public links on the self-hosted service are long-lived; guest links are limited to a 10-minute trial.
6.3 The service runs on a single self-hosted instance and may be briefly unavailable during maintenance or failures.
