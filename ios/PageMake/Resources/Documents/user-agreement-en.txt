Page-make User Agreement

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. Service Description

Page-make is a web and Node service hosting tool: you upload a zip or individual HTML/CSS/JS files, or create an npm / pnpm project, and we extract, check, install dependencies, build, and generate a publicly accessible link. Standard users can use static deployment; npm / pnpm backend deployment is available only to VIP and above. The service is free for signed-in users.

2. Accounts

2.1 This service supports Apple, Google, and guest access. VIP, SVIP, and Ultra are benefit levels for an existing account; they do not create a separate account or password. Accounts are for personal use only.
2.2 Tier benefits are adjusted by an administrator and are not enabled automatically. This service offers no paid option. Standard guests may submit a request from Settings > Upgrade to VIP for human review. If a request is declined, the reason is shown and you may revise and resubmit.
2.3 You are responsible for all activity under your account.

3.6 Any other unlawful content.

4. Enforcement

4.1 Violating projects will be taken down (the link shows "taken down"); related files may be retained temporarily for review, appeals, and compliance handling.
4.2 Violating accounts are banned by level: first minor violation — 1 day; repeat violations — 1 week; fraud or phishing — 1 month; pornography, terrorism, or malware — permanent.
4.3 Reports are reviewed manually. If you believe an action was taken in error, you may appeal via the feedback channel.

6.2 Public links created by signed-in users remain available while the project exists; standard guest links are limited to a 10-minute trial. Links created by VIP and above remain available while the project exists.

6.4 npm / pnpm projects are subject to dependency-install time, build time, runtime memory, and concurrency limits; projects may be temporarily unavailable when limits are exceeded or a process crashes.
