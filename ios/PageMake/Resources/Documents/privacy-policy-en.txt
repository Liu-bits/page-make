Page-make Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account information: when you sign in with Apple, we receive your Apple user identifier (User ID) and email (if you choose to share it); when you sign in with Google, we receive the corresponding account identifier and email; guest access uses a random identifier generated on your device. VIP, SVIP, and Ultra are benefit levels for an existing Apple, Google, or guest account, not separate sign-in methods.
1.2 Uploaded content: archives, HTML/CSS/JS files, npm / pnpm project source (including package.json), and their extracted contents.
1.3 Usage logs: timestamps and results of operations such as upload, deploy and link refresh, used for troubleshooting.
1.4 Feedback: messages and reports you submit via Settings > Feedback.
1.5 Credentials: session credentials are stored in the device's secure storage. The administrator password is stored only as a salted, irreversible hash; no plaintext password is stored.
1.6 Upgrade request: the optional description (up to 300 characters) you enter when submitting a request from Settings > Upgrade to VIP, along with the submission and review timestamps. The description is used only for human review of your intended use.

2. How We Use Information

2.1 To provide web and Node service hosting: extracting archives, checking your code, installing dependencies declared by your project, running build scripts, starting services, and generating public links. npm / pnpm projects run only in a restricted server subprocess after you publish them; executable files are not downloaded to your iPhone.
2.2 To troubleshoot issues, maintain your signed-in session, and keep the service safe (e.g. banning abusive accounts, taking down violating projects).
2.3 To let authorized personnel review VIP upgrade requests and decide whether to adjust account benefits.
2.4 To comply with applicable laws and respond to lawful law-enforcement requests.
2.5 We do not use your information for advertising and do not sell it to any third party.

3. Where Information Is Stored

3.1 Your account information, files, and project data are stored only on the servers operated for this service.
3.2 Please note: public links are accessible to anyone while valid. Do not upload files containing sensitive information.
3.3 When you delete a project, its files are deleted immediately. Projects taken down for policy violations may be retained temporarily for review, appeals, and compliance handling.
3.4 Incomplete temporary uploads expire and are cleaned up after 30 minutes.
3.5 Account data is generally retained while your account exists. When you delete your account, the account record, projects, and files are deleted unless retention is required by law. VIP upgrade requests are removed together with your account; already-reviewed records keep their review outcome for dispute handling.

4. Information Sharing

4.1 Contracted infrastructure providers may access the account and file data necessary to operate the service.
4.2 We do not disclose your information to any other third party unless required by law.

5. Tracking and Advertising

This app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

6. Your Rights

6.1 You can delete your projects and their files at any time within the app.
6.2 You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings > Delete Account.
6.3 You can sign out at any time (Settings > Sign Out).

7. Data Security

7.1 The administrator password is protected with a salted hash, session credentials are stored in the device's secure storage, and data is transmitted over encrypted connections.
7.2 Administrative access is restricted to authorized personnel, and account deletion, bans, and takedowns require human action.
7.3 The npm / pnpm runtime gives project processes only the minimal environment needed to start and does not inherit server secrets or sign-in credentials.
7.4 If you discover a data-security issue, please contact us through in-app feedback.

8. Protection of Minors

This service is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their information. Minors should use the service under guardian supervision.

9. Policy Updates

If this policy changes materially, we will update this document in the app and update the effective date.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us via Settings > Feedback in the app.
